title=Last Stand Together
description=[h1]A multiplayer version of Project Zomboid’s Last Stand mode.[/h1]
description=[h2]This mod lets you set up a Last Stand survival zone in multiplayer (or singleplayer). You and your friends pick a building, and waves of zombies will attack. The goal is simple: survive as long as you can.[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Instructions:[/b] In Singplayer or in MP as admin/host (or debug mode) hit escape and view the Setup Panel. Select a building in Project Zomboid to be your base of operations.
description=
description=Full (and automatic) integration with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2853615523]Shops and Traders[/url] (see: required items). Shops arranged using Shops and Traders will automatically fill the building's most container-heavy room.
description=
description=Zombies wave population, intervals, and more can all be configured in the panel or in sandbox options.
